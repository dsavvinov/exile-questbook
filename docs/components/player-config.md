# PlayerConfig

Stores and persists the player's settings.

Parent: Application.

## What it does

Holds the settings the app has to remember between sessions.

The managed filter selection is the first of them: picking a filter has to
survive a restart, or the player picks it again every time they open the app.
There will be more settings.
