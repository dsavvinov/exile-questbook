# StashScanner

Scans the player's stash tabs, producing the stash part of player state.

Parent: Application.

## What it does

Reads the player's stash tabs and produces `PlayerStashesState`, the observable
half of the player's state. Smart presets are applied and unapplied off the back
of it.
